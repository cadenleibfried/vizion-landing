Owners always ask

Fair questions, straight answers.

What does it cost?
A one-time setup fee, then a flat fee per qualified booked appointment, priced by industry. No retainer, no percentage of your revenue. Exact numbers get quoted on the call and locked in writing, and they don't change mid-campaign.
Who pays for the ads?
You fund your own ad account and every dollar goes straight to the ad platform. None of it passes through us. Your money buys ads. Our fee is earned per qualified booking.
What about no-shows?
You pay nothing that week. And if we don't put 3 qualified appointments on your calendar in the first 45 days of live ads, the setup fee comes back. We carry the performance risk. That's the point of the model.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. Month to month, cancel anytime with written notice. We keep clients by performing, not by trapping them in paper.
How fast do we launch?
Your funnel, ads, and follow-up system are typically built within a week of kickoff. Once you approve everything, ads go live and the guarantee clock starts that day.
Will I actually know what's going on?
Always. You get a weekly plain-English report, no jargon, plus a live dashboard showing every lead, every appointment, and every dollar of ad spend in real time. Hands off never means in the dark.
